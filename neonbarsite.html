Your Neon-lit community

Born in Kerala, built for the world. Neon Bar is where Malayali culture meets the internet — memes, movies, music, tech, and everything in between. Speak Malayalam, English, or whatever — you're welcome here.

  • A vibrant Malayali-at-heart community
  • Open to all languages and backgrounds
  • Chill conversations, zero toxicity
  • Active members, real connections
  • Events, games, and collabs
